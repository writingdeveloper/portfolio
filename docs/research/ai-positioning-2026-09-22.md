## Decision and limits

Keep AI-native product engineering / agent systems as the portfolio specialization, backed by TypeScript/Python, product, backend and infrastructure work. This is an editorial fit decision, not a measured ranking of the whole hiring market. One live posting and three official engineering articles are a limited sample. Articles explain practice, not job-market share or the author's skills.

- [OpenAI: Harness engineering](https://openai.com/index/harness-engineering/) — published 2026-02-11. Reference for repository legibility, acceptance boundaries and inspectable testing. It does not establish universal productivity gains.

Separates transcripts from actual end-state outcomes and deterministic, model and human grading. Supports checking artifacts instead of trusting a success report.
- [Anthropic: Effective context engineering](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents) — published 2025-09-29. Foundation for context/tool selection, not a new September 2026 announcement.
